# Data attribution and redistribution

The data source is the United Nations Statistics Division, UN Comtrade. The included extract contains 120 records retrieved on 26 September 2026. It is a frozen research snapshot, not a live feed or a replacement for the source database.

UN Comtrade retains rights in its data. Its [policy on use and re-dissemination](https://uncomtrade.org/docs/policy-on-use-and-re-dissemination/) permits free analytical applications and small extracts within its stated exceptions, including extracts of up to 100,000 records. This project uses 120 records and a small number of derived tables and figures. Consult the source terms before extending or commercializing redistribution. Do not represent the data as MIT-licensed.

Original code is separately MIT-licensed. Downloaded journal manuscripts and institutional reports are not redistributed in this repository. Links and original bibliographic and analytical notes are supplied instead. No confidential, personal, restricted, or synthetic data enter the substantive outputs.
