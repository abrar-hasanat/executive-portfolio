## Version discrepancy that changes interpretation

The University of Washington departmental copy named `bch.pdf`, dated June 2023, uses a synthetic-control design and reports adverse wage effects. It differs materially from the publication-linked March 2023 manuscript and the published article abstract, which describe triple differences and positive wage and working-condition estimates. The publication-linked version is used for the final article's findings. The other file is retained privately as a version audit, not cited as a separate independent study or combined with the final results. A date on a file is not sufficient evidence that it is the final published analysis.

## Where apparent disagreements come from

Heath and Mobarak study access to jobs before the disaster. Bossavie and colleagues study a bundle of responses across labor markets. Boudreau randomizes one enforcement intervention several years later. Their outcomes, periods, comparison units and estimands differ. Positive job-access effects, improvements for covered workers, and vulnerability among subcontracting workers can all coexist. The departmental/final Bossavie discrepancy is a version issue; district-control sensitivity within the final paper is a substantive identification issue.

Boudreau's experiment does not establish that all safety investments have no employment cost. Its gross-wage outcome is the factory wage bill, not the individual hourly wage measure used by Bossavie and colleagues. Null estimates have finite precision. Its safety index mixes physical checks and awareness; the experiment cannot directly estimate effects on rare catastrophic deaths.

## Primary institutional materials

The original 2013 Accord is used for the content of contractual commitments, with no inference of compliance from a signed clause. USTR and CBP establish US preference timing and use. The 2013 European Parliament resolution supplies period-appropriate EU context. UN Comtrade methodology governs data interpretation. USITC publication 5543, Table 5.5, provides an external scale and direction check, with definition and vintage differences explicitly retained.
